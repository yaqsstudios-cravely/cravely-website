Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Operated by Yaqs Studios (a sole trader in the United Kingdom) · cravely.support@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account: email address, name, username, date of birth (asked when you sign up, only to check you're 13 or older; it's never shown on your profile), a password hash (argon2id; we never see your password), and your Apple or Google account ID if you sign in with them.
  • Content: photos, captions, ratings, tags, comments, reactions, follows and blocks.
  • Location (optional): only if you allow it. It is used to tag where a post was taken (with "Tag my location", "Use current location" or the map pin), to show the places nearest to you first when you search for a restaurant (your approximate position is sent to our place search provider for that search only), to show craves near you, and to move your map buddy while the map is open. Your live position is never shown to other people, and we don't track your location in the background or when the map is closed.
  • Phone number (optional): if you add one, we keep a keyed one-way hash of it and its last 3 digits, so people who have your number can find you. You can turn this off or remove your number in Settings > Privacy and safety.
  • Contacts (optional): only if you tap "Search your contacts" and allow access. The app reads just the phone numbers in your contacts and sends them to our server, which checks them against the numbers people have added and throws them away. We don't store your contacts or use them for anything else.
  • Feedback and error reports: what you write in Send feedback, and, when something goes wrong in the app, the error message, the screen you were on, your phone model and the app version, so we can fix it.
  • Purchases: what you bought and transaction IDs from Apple, Google or Stripe. We never receive your full card details.
  • Technical: IP address and request logs for security and rate limiting, kept for up to 30 days.

How we use it

To run the Service, show your posts to the audience you choose, prevent abuse, process purchases, and answer support requests.

Who we share it with

Service providers acting for us: hosting (Render), database (Neon), image storage and CDN (Cloudinary), automatic photo checks for nudity (Sightengine, which receives each photo you post only to check it), GIF search in comments (GIPHY, which receives your search words through our server, never your identity), email (Resend), payments (Stripe, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) and place search and maps (OpenStreetMap services). We don't sell personal data.

Optional rewarded ads

If you choose to watch an ad for a reward, the ad is shown by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure the ad, and, if you agree in the consent prompt, to personalise ads. We pass Google your Cravely user ID and the reward you picked only so that Google can confirm to us that you finished the ad. You can refuse personalised ads in the consent prompt, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or simply never tap “Watch an ad”. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices and rights

Edit your profile, change post visibility, make your account private, block users and delete posts at any time. Delete your account in Settings > Account > Delete account: your profile, posts, photos, comments, reactions and sign-in credentials are deleted, and Sign in with Apple tokens are revoked. Purchase records are kept only where tax law requires. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or object to processing; email us to use them.

Retention

Records of moderation decisions (suspensions, bans and removals) are kept to enforce them. If a moderator deletes an account for breaking the rules, we keep a one-way hash of its email address so it can't be used to sign up again. Deleted posts stay in Recently deleted for 7 days, then are removed permanently. Unverified sign-ups are removed after 24 hours.

Children

Cravely is for people aged 13 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and don't create accounts for anyone younger. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it. If you think a child under 13 is using Cravely, email us.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, your session is stored in the device keychain, and access to production data is restricted.
