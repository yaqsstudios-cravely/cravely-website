Community Guidelines

Effective 10 October 2026 · Operated by Yaqs Studios (a sole trader in the United Kingdom) · cravely.support@gmail.com

Cravely is for sharing food you love. Keep it kind, honest and safe. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves.
  • Hate speech or symbols targeting people for who they are.
  • Nudity, sexual content, graphic violence or gore.
  • Spam, scams, fake reviews or impersonation.
  • Posting someone's private home or personal details.
  • Illegal goods or dangerous activities.

How we enforce

Comments and captions are screened automatically. Every post, comment and account has a Report option, and you can block anyone. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours, and content with several reports is hidden while it's reviewed. Violations lead to removal; serious or repeated ones lead to a permanent ban.

Report something

Tap ⋯ on a post or comment, or Report on a profile. For urgent safety issues email cravely.support@gmail.com.
